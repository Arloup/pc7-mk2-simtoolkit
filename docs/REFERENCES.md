# References

Third-party documents used during the project. They are **not redistributed in this
repository**: each one belongs to its authors or publishers, so the list below links to
the official source instead.

The full bibliography is in the TM report (`TM_PartII.pdf`, Bibliographie).

## Aerodynamics fundamentals

- Anderson, J. D. Jr. (2001). *Fundamentals of Aerodynamics*, 3rd edition. McGraw-Hill.
  ISBN 0-07-237335-0. Copyrighted textbook, available from the publisher or libraries.
- Federal Aviation Administration (2023). *Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge*,
  FAA-H-8083-25C. <https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak>
- Manningham, D. *Flying Angle of Attack*. Business and Commercial Aviation (magazine
  article, September issue). Copyrighted; not available online from the publisher.
